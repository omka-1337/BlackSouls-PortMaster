Game Information
Title: BLACK SOULS
URL: https://store.steampowered.com/app/1424910/BLACK_SOULS/

Instructions:
Not ready to run, and the game is adult content. BLACK SOULS is a paid title, so
this ships the engine only and you need your own copy to test. Copy Audio/,
Graphics/, Fonts/, Game.ini and Game.rgss3a into the blacksouls folder, alongside
the engine. Game.exe, System/ and installscript.vdf are Windows only. Keep both
Game.rgss3a and the loose Graphics folder: nine assets exist in both, and the loose
copies are the English release's replacements.

Engine is mkxp-z (RPG Maker VX Ace / RGSS3), GLES, no gl4es. Input goes through
SDL_GameController natively, so nothing is remapped and the bundled .ini is
deliberately blank; gptokeyb2 runs only for the Start+Select exit hotkey. The game
renders at 640x480.

Tested on my RG40XX H (Knulli, H700 / Mali-G31, 1GB): boots, plays, battles and
saves all work, peak resident set around 233 MB. It is the only device I own, so
every other CFW below is open. Help welcome.

One Knulli oddity, likely not port related: a few seconds after exiting, the
firmware runs its own save routine and draws a progress screen. Relaunching inside
that window leaves both drawing at once and the picture tears. Waiting it out
clears it. Please say if another CFW does the same.
