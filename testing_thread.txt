Game Information
Title: BLACK SOULS
URL: https://store.steampowered.com/app/1424910/BLACK_SOULS/

Instructions:
The game is a paid title, so the engine ships on its own. Copy Audio/, Graphics/,
Fonts/, Game.ini and Game.rgss3a from your own installation into the blacksouls
folder, alongside the engine. Game.exe, System/ and installscript.vdf are Windows
only and are not needed.

Engine is mkxp-z, so input goes through SDL_GameController natively and nothing is
remapped. gptokeyb2 runs only for the Start+Select exit hotkey.

| Button | Action |
|--|--|
|A| Confirm |
|B| Cancel, open menu |
|X| Dash |
|D-Pad| Move, menu navigation |
|Start| Open menu |
